Bug fixes:

• Fixed a crash that could permanently block the app from opening. A task with a degenerate time range broke the timeline and made every launch fail (#154).
• A task ending exactly at midnight is no longer treated as an overnight task and no longer appears as a 0-minute entry on the next day.
• Existing schedules are repaired automatically on update — no data loss and no reinstall needed.
• Task progress can no longer become invalid for very short tasks.
• The editor now rejects reversed and zero-length time ranges.
